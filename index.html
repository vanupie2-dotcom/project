# About Me — Tanisha Hello! My name is Tanisha, and I am a passionate and enthusiastic student currently pursuing a Diploma in Computer Science. Along with my academic studies, I am learning web development through HARTRON, where I am working on improving my computer knowledge, technical abilities, and programming skills. I have a strong interest in technology and enjoy discovering how websites, software, and digital applications are designed and developed. I believe that technology is an important part of our modern world because it helps people solve problems, communicate, share information, and turn innovative ideas into reality. My interest in computers motivates me to explore new concepts, learn different programming techniques, and understand how technology can be used to create useful and meaningful digital experiences. As an aspiring web developer, I am learning the fundamental technologies of web development, including HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. HTML helps me create the structure of web pages, CSS allows me to design attractive layouts and improve the visual appearance of websites, and JavaScript helps me add functionality and interactive features. By learning these technologies, I am gradually developing the skills required to build responsive, modern, and user-friendly websites. My learning journey is not limited to understanding theoretical concepts. I also enjoy practising coding and creating small projects that help me apply my knowledge in practical situations. Projects such as a personal portfolio website, a calculator, and a task management application provide opportunities to understand website structure, styling, user interaction, and programming logic. Each project helps me identify areas where I can improve and encourages me to explore more advanced concepts. One of my main interests is designing websites that are visually attractive, easy to navigate, and comfortable for users. I enjoy experimenting with colours, layouts, typography, animations, and responsive designs to understand how different elements work together to create a professional website. I also want to improve my ability to write clean, organised, and understandable code so that my projects become easier to maintain and develop further. I consider myself a motivated learner who believes in consistency, patience, creativity, and continuous improvement. I understand that learning programming requires practice, logical thinking, and the ability to learn from mistakes. Whenever I encounter a coding challenge, I try to understand the problem, explore possible solutions, and improve my knowledge through practice. I believe that every mistake can become an opportunity to learn something new. My Diploma in Computer Science is helping me build an academic foundation in the field of computers, while my web development learning gives me an opportunity to explore my creative and technical interests. Through my studies and practical projects, I aim to develop a better understanding of programming, website development, computer applications, and modern digital technologies. Apart from learning technical skills, I also want to develop important professional qualities such as communication, teamwork, time management, problem-solving, and adaptability. I believe these qualities are valuable for working on projects, sharing ideas, understanding user requirements, and collaborating with other people in the technology industry. My long-term goal is to become a skilled and confident web developer who can design and develop professional websites and useful web applications. I want to continue learning modern technologies, explore new development tools, work on more challenging projects, and gain practical experience that helps me grow in the IT field. I also hope to build a strong portfolio that represents my learning journey, creativity, dedication, and technical progress. I know that becoming a successful developer takes time, effort, and continuous learning. Therefore, I am focused on strengthening my fundamentals, practising regularly, exploring new ideas, and improving my projects step by step. I am excited about the opportunities that technology offers and look forward to expanding my knowledge as I progress in my career. This website represents a part of my journey as a Computer Science student and an aspiring web developer. It is a place where I can introduce myself, share my interests, showcase my practice projects, and demonstrate the skills I am developing. I hope to continue growing, learning, and creating digital experiences that combine creativity, functionality, and simplicity. **My philosophy is simple: Keep learning, keep practising, stay curious, and never stop improving.** Every small step I take today brings me closer to my future goals.
